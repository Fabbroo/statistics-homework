From Wallet Seed to Ethereum Address

Homework 1 - ECC Demo Homework 2 - Wallet Pipeline

Analysis of the cryptographic pipeline used in Ethereum wallets.

Overview

Ethereum wallets transform a mnemonic phrase into an Ethereum address through a sequence of cryptographic standards and primitives. 

Mnemonic
    ↓ BIP-39
Seed
    ↓ BIP-32 / BIP-44
Private Key
    ↓ secp256k1
Public Key
    ↓ Keccak-256
Ethereum Address
    ↓ EIP-55
Checksummed Address

BIP-39

Mnemonic sentence generation and conversion into a seed.

BIP-32

Hierarchical deterministic key derivation.

BIP-44

Standard derivation paths and wallet organization.

secp256k1

Public-key cryptography and digital signatures.

Keccak-256

Hashing and Ethereum address derivation.

EIP-55

Mixed-case checksum addresses.

Historical Timeline

  • 1977 - RSA
  • 2001 - SHA-2 / SHA-512
  • 2012 - BIP-32
  • 2013 - BIP-39
  • 2014 - BIP-44
  • 2015 - SHA-3
  • 2016 - EIP-55

Discussion

Why Ethereum uses multiple cryptographic techniques instead of a single algorithm.
